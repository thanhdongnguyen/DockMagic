## Visual review

- Aether Crystal: the most overtly magical, with mineral facets and a suspended
  central sapphire; ornate surface detail will need simplification for tiny sizes.
- Arcane Porcelain: refined sculptural arches and a clear progression from seed
  to assembled structure. A strong starting point for the requested balance of
  mystical and modern; curved shading will not translate exactly into solid SVG.
- Obsidian Orbit: the most industrial and futuristic; broad interlocking rings
  establish the progression. The large metallic reflections are raster material
  treatment, not resolution-independent vector geometry.

All three are exploration images at 1536 × 1024. Their boards and quiet labels
are presentation only. A selected direction should be regenerated as separate
transparent badge assets and checked at 48/58/112/185 pt before production.
The current concept boards do not prove small-size legibility. No colored aura,
rainbow palette, service logo, or product UI color was added.
